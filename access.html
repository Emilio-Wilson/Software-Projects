Access & Credentialing Software Application

HTMLCSSJavaScriptMicrosoft FormsPower AutomateSharePoint
Project Overview ⌄

This is the Access and Credentialing application for Charleston International Airport, used by contractors, vendors, and new employees who need an airport issued badge, an escort required ID, or a vehicle or parking permit. The site organizes every credential CHS issues alongside the forms and requirements tied to each one.

Project Goal ⌄

The goal is to let an applicant walk through the entire process on their own: selecting the right credential type, completing the application, passing the required background check, and scheduling an appointment to receive the physical badge, without needing to call or visit the office first.

Tools Used ⌄

The solution uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create a user friendly microsite that provides easy access to required forms and allows users to submit and print completed forms.

Microsoft Forms is used to collect contractor, vendor, and employee information and form submissions. When a Microsoft Form is submitted, Power Automate automatically triggers a workflow to process the submission and save the collected information to a designated SharePoint Site.

SharePoint serves as the centralized location for storing and managing reports, submitted forms, files, credentialing documentation, and personal information. This allows the Access and Credentialing Department to efficiently organize, access, and manage information for contractors, vendors, and employees.

Together, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, and SharePoint provide an integrated solution that simplifies form access and submission while automating data management and workflow processes.

No code reference.

Success ⌄

Applicants can identify the right credential, download the correct form, understand the background check requirement, and book an appointment without a phone call or an office visit. This has cut down the repeat questions the credentialing office previously fielded manually, and given the office a structured, trackable appointment record instead of a paper calendar.
