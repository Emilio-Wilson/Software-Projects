Emilio Wilson, a native of Charleston, South Carolina, is an Engineer and IT professional with a passion for technology, innovation, and problem solving. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Engineering with a concentration in Innovation Design from Coastal Carolina University in 2023. During college, Emilio gained valuable experience as a football team member and intern with the Charleston International Airport Aviation Authority, where he transitioned into the IT Department. After graduating, he joined the Aviation Authority as an IT Technician and, within five months, was promoted to IT Application Support Analyst. In this role, Emilio develops and supports software applications while applying his engineering and technical expertise to create innovative solutions. He continues to grow in the fields of engineering, technology, and software development while using his skills to solve real world challenges.
